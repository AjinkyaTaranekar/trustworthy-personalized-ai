AI Scrutability and User Control
Owen discussed his interest in scrutinability, which refers to a user's ability to understand both what and why an AI system is doing something. He explained that this goes beyond simple explainability, focusing on how users can interact with and control AI systems, particularly when dealing with proactive actions. Owen used the analogy of driving a car in different modes to illustrate how users interact with systems in different ways based on their intent, emphasizing the complexity of understanding AI capabilities, especially in dialogue-based interactions with LLMs.

LLMs' Lack of Explainable Reasoning
Owen discussed the limitations of LLMs in providing explainable reasoning, using the example of recommending a Frappuccino. He compared modern LLMs to expert systems from the past, highlighting that while LLMs can generate convincing responses, they lack the ability to expose their internal logical structure. Owen described LLMs as "sociopaths" in this context, as they will provide any answer based on statistical data without genuine understanding or introspection.
