Constitutional Principles and Evolution
Ajinkya and Owen discussed the challenge of maintaining adherence to constitutional principles as they grow and evolve over time. AI LLM Testing Challenges Discussion
Owen and Ajinkya discussed the challenges of testing and probing AI LLMs to detect constitution drift. Owen distinguished between probes, which are internal registers for observation, and tests, which involve direct interaction with the LLM. He emphasized the need to carefully navigate potential circular logic when using LLMs to test other LLMs, suggesting this as a key consideration for Ajinkya's phase two work.
