Semester Group Name Confusion
Owen and Ajinkya discussed the start of the semester and the confusion caused by certain group names in Owen's module, including Pikachu, Blanc, Colombo, and others. Owen mentioned adding a new team, Kojak, due to new module participants.

Recruitment and Project Progress Updates
Owen discussed his current workload of reviewing 28 CVs for a senior project management role, aiming to interview 3-5 candidates. He emphasized the importance of finding candidates who not only have the necessary project management skills but also demonstrate understanding of the topic area and can work effectively in complex multi-partner consortiums. Ajinkya shared his progress on creating a dataset and training a model for a chat assistant project, which included handling mathematical calculations and weather queries. Both participants discussed the challenges of job recruitment and the complexities of making career decisions, particularly when balancing immediate opportunities with long-term goals.

Generative Agent Behavior Analysis
Ajinkya and Owen discussed the behavior of a generative agent performing calculations. Ajinkya explained that the agent sometimes produced incorrect results but could be corrected by writing a Python script for the calculations. Owen suggested studying behaviorism to understand the agent's responses without inspecting its internal functioning. They compared the agent's behavior to psychological tests, noting that while the agent could hold "dark thoughts," its actions were constrained by post-hoc training and societal norms.

Enhancing LLMs for Mathematical Tasks
Owen and Ajinkya discussed the limitations and potential improvements of large language models (LLMs) in handling mathematical and knowledge-oriented tasks. Owen emphasized the need to focus on post-hoc solutions to address LLM weaknesses, drawing inspiration from behaviorism and human cognitive processes. They explored the idea of using context retention and efficient resource utilization to keep LLMs within their appropriate capabilities. Ajinkya shared plans to experiment with context-saving mechanisms and a recursive language model to improve LLM performance. Owen suggested taking a step back to clearly define the research focus and experimental setup, emphasizing the importance of framing the work within the existing literature. They agreed to reconvene in a few weeks after Ajinkya develops a clearer research direction.
