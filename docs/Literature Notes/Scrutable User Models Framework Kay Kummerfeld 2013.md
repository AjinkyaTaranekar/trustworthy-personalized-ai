## What is known (from citation in LLNCS paper and topic pages)

- 20+ year old foundational paper establishing the scrutability framework for user models.
- Defines "open user model": the system's model of the user is visible to and editable by the user.
- Five problems that map onto LLM over-personalisation failures (from the LLNCS paper's UMAP tradition section).
- Established the research tradition continued by Jeromela & Conlan 2024, Akbar & Conlan 2024, Ramos et al. 2024, TEARS.

## Thesis Relevance

This is the primary theoretical ancestor of the scrutable-user-model design goal in the thesis. The 5W+H schema and its user-inspectable representation directly operationalise Kay & Kummerfeld's scrutability requirement. Supervisor Conlan's group works in this tradition — knowing this paper well is important for supervisor meetings.
