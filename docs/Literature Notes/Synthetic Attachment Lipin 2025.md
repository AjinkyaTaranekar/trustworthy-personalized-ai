## What is known (from citation in security analysis paper)

- Documents that emotionally reactive AI systems produce attachment behaviours in users that are functionally similar to human relationships.
- Key claim cited in the thesis: parasocial bonds form even when users know the system is AI; disclosing AI identity is not sufficient to prevent dependency.
- Argues the system should actively monitor for and interrupt dependency formation.

## Thesis Relevance

Empirical support for the emotional-dependency ethical risk in the empathy topic page. The thesis's autonomy-preserving constraint (model should redirect users to better consultants, surface uncertainty) is motivated in part by this finding.
