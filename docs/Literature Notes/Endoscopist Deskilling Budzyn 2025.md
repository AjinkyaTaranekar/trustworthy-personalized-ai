## What is known (from citation in security analysis paper)

- Clinical study documenting that endoscopists who habitually use AI-assisted detection show measurable decline in independent adenoma detection capability.
- Empirical evidence for OWASP LLM09 (Overreliance) in a high-stakes clinical setting.
- The thesis cites this as the strongest empirical evidence that AI-induced deskilling is real and measurable — not just theoretical.

## Thesis Relevance

Provides the empirical anchor for the deskilling ethical risk in the empathy and security analysis papers. Motivates the thesis's autonomy-preserving design constraint: the model should surface uncertainty and redirect rather than providing confident answers that users stop verifying independently.
