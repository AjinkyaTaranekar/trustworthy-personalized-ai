## What is known (from citation in LLNCS paper)

- EMNLP 2024 Findings paper by Zheng et al. evaluating persona injection in system prompts.
- 162 roles × 2,410 questions; result: persona injection is useless or actively harmful for objective tasks.
- Supports the thesis's claim that coarse over-personalisation (applying user persona to every response) degrades quality on non-personal queries.

## Thesis Relevance

Empirical evidence for the "intent override" failure mode — the model applies persona context even when irrelevant. Confirms that the selective memory injection approach (Self-ReCheck, RPEval L2) is necessary.
