## What is known (from citation in security analysis paper)

- Extends the "extended mind" thesis (Clark & Chalmers) in a critical direction: frictionless AI offloading bypasses the effort required to build durable, internal understanding.
- The "friction" of solving problems yourself is what consolidates learning — AI that removes friction removes the learning.
- Relevant to: OWASP LLM09 (Overreliance), deskilling ethical risk.

## Thesis Relevance

Provides the philosophical framing for the deskilling argument: it is not just that users become dependent on AI outputs (Budzyń et al. 2025), but that the frictionless quality of AI assistance specifically prevents the formation of durable skills. The thesis's autonomy-preserving constraint should introduce appropriate friction rather than optimising purely for user satisfaction.
